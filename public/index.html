The problem

A failed attempt costs twice.

The fee is gone the moment you file — approved or rejected. Then comes the agent, the travel, the day off work… and you file again.

And sometimes the game is rigged: at many centers, agents know an application will fail — a missing Gazette, a name mismatch, a blown lifetime limit — and file it anyway to collect their form-filling charges. Your money and time, wasted on an application that was doomed before it was filed. EkSudhaar takes that power away: you see every failure point before anyone touches your application.
